About EmotionDetect

EmotionDetect is a full-stack facial emotion inference platform using a FER model pipeline, production-ready APIs, and real-time browser workflows.

  • 7 emotion classes: angry, disgust, fear, happy, neutral, sad, surprise.
  • Upload and live-camera inference support.
  • Prediction history tracking with confidence and latency.
